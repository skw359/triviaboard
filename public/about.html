About Trivia Center

A trivia game that pulls questions from the Open Trivia Database, tracks scores, and posts them to a global leaderboard.

How to Play

  • First, enter your name and pick a category and difficulty!
  • Answer 10 multiple-choice questions
  • Submit your score to the leaderboard!

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, Vanilla JS
  • Backend: Node.js + Express
  • Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
  • External API: Open Trivia DB
  • Libraries: Chart.js, canvas-confetti
  • Deployed on: Vercel
